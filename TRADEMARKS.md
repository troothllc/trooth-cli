# The Trooth name

Trooth is a trademark of Trooth, LLC. The full policy is at [trooth.co/trademark-policy](https://trooth.co/trademark-policy). In short:

- You may say that your software implements, reads or checks the Trooth record format, and link to the specification.
- You may call a statement you issue a Trooth record only when your implementation passes the conformance suite in `tests/vectors/` and you are listed in the public registry of issuers at [trooth.co/standard/issuers.json](https://trooth.co/standard/issuers.json). To be listed, write to legal@trooth.co with your implementation's address and the suite's output.
- You may not use the Trooth name or logo in a way that suggests Trooth, LLC made, signed or endorses your statements, software or company.
